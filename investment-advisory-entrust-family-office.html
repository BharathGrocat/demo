Frequently asked questions

I already work with several wealth managers. Why would I need you?+

Most families we advise already have relationships with banks and wealth managers. We add an independent, fee-only layer: one consolidated view across all of them, advice that is not tied to any product, and a check of every proposal you receive against your Investment Policy Statement. Many clients keep their existing relationships and use us as their co-pilot and gatekeeper.

How involved is your senior team?+

Each family works with a senior adviser as its main point of contact, and recommendations are vetted by our Chief Investment Officer and Investment Committee before they reach you. Senior members of the team take part in your reviews.

How do you evaluate the products you recommend?+

Every product goes through our research team’s due diligence on track record, process, costs, liquidity and risks, and is vetted by our CIO and Investment Committee. Because we are fee-only and earn no commissions, a product is recommended only when it fits your goals and your IPS.

Can you advise on global investments?+

Yes. We advise on global diversification, including investing abroad under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme and through GIFT City, and on how global assets fit alongside your Indian portfolio, tax position and liquidity needs.

What does a SEBI-registered investment adviser do?+

An investment adviser registered with SEBI gives personalised investment advice for a fee, under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013. Advice is based on your financial situation, objectives and risk profile.

How is investment advisory different from wealth management?+

Investment advisory is focused on advice: we recommend, and you decide. “Wealth management” is a broader term that often also covers selling or distributing investment products.

Can you review my existing portfolio?+

Yes. We look at your holdings across institutions and asset classes, along with your allocation, concentration and liquidity, and how well they match your goals.

Do you advise NRIs?+

We work with NRIs who hold investments in India, subject to the regulations of their country of residence. Tell us your country in the form and we’ll confirm how we can help.

What should I bring to the first consultation?+

A broad picture of your current investments, your goals and time horizon, and any upcoming commitments. Statements are useful but not needed for the first conversation.
