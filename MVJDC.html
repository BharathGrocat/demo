About the fest

Celebrating Talent, Leadership & Innovation

UTSAV 2026 is a two-day inter-collegiate cultural and management fest organised by MVJ Degree College, Bengaluru. Students from degree colleges across Bengaluru and Karnataka, along with PU and engineering colleges, come together to showcase talent, creativity, leadership, managerial skills and sportsmanship. It ends with a Grand DJ Night and the Valedictory Ceremony.

1
A common platform to showcase student talent and creativity.
2
Promote leadership, entrepreneurship and managerial excellence.
3
Strengthen the visibility and brand image of MVJ Degree College.
4
Enhance student engagement and institutional outreach.

FAQ

Good to know


Who can participate?

Students of degree colleges, and PU and engineering colleges, from Bengaluru and across Karnataka.

When and where is UTSAV 2026?

2–3 December 2026 at MVJ Degree College, Channasandra, Near ITPB, Whitefield, Bengaluru 560067.

How much does it cost?

Solo events ₹200, duet and small teams ₹300, groups of 5–12 ₹500–₹700 (final fee to be confirmed).

How big can a team be?

Small team events allow up to 3 members. Group Dance, Group Song, Street Play and Mad Ads allow 5–12.

Can I enter more than one event?

Yes, subject to the schedule. Each event has its own registration fee.

Where can I find the event rules?

Open any event card in the Events section for its rules and details.

What should I carry?

Your college ID card and registration confirmation.

Who do I contact for questions?

Use the enquiry form below, or contact the event coordinator listed on each event card.
