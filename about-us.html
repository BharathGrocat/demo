About Me

Dr. L. Ramachandran

Photographer. Filmmaker. Cinematographer. Author.

Dr. L. Ramachandran is a self-taught photographer, filmmaker, cinematographer, director, documentarian and author whose work is rooted in visual storytelling, culture, people and the preservation of stories through powerful imagery.

Hailing from Valangaiman near Thiruvarur, Tamil Nadu, he has built a creative journey spanning more than two decades. His work has taken him across more than 50 countries, where he has documented people, cultures, traditions, art, architecture, sporting events and significant moments through photography and film.

His distinctive approach combines observation, emotion, light and composition to create photographs and films that go beyond documentation — capturing the character of a moment, the expression of a person and the cultural context surrounding a story.

The Lifeline

A Journey Rooted in Storytelling

For L. Ramachandran, photography is more than the act of capturing an image. It has been a lifelong journey of exploration, documentation, mentorship and social contribution — a way of preserving people, places, emotions and cultural memory.

01
The Beginning

Roots in Valangaiman

A self-taught photographer from Valangaiman near Thiruvarur, he set out with a camera and a curiosity about people and their stories — a journey that would take him across more than 50 countries.

Rather than restricting himself to a single photographic identity, he explored the entire spectrum of visual storytelling — from forests and mountains to temples and heritage sites, from sporting arenas to fashion studios, and from public figures to ordinary people whose stories deserve to be seen.

02
Social Contribution

Photography as a Responsibility

For Ramachandran, the camera is not only a creative instrument. It is a medium through which society can be documented, represented and remembered — giving visibility to communities and individuals who may otherwise remain outside the mainstream visual narrative.

He has photographed more than 25 individuals from the transgender and third-gender community, presenting them not through stereotypes, but through identity, dignity, confidence, personality and beauty.

20 transgender people were showcased as professional fashion models at an international gallery showcase in Miami, as part of Medley of Art.
03
Culture & Heritage

Supporting Traditional and Folk Artists

He has documented the lives, art forms, costumes, expressions and performances of folk artists and traditional performers — through photographic bodies of work, exhibitions and calendar projects that help preserve a fading culture.

The Gyeongju Art Fair 2023 in South Korea showcased folk artists specialising in Mayilaatam, Oyilaatam, Therukoothu, Siva Thaandavam and Karagattam. The proceeds were granted to the folk artists, with coverage in publications such as The Korea Times.

An exclusive Therukoothu calendar — his first self-made calendar — was launched in association with actor Vijay Sethupathi, drawing national press attention to the hardships of these artistes.

04
Giving Back

A Mentor to the Next Generation

He has trained 300+ photographers and visual creators, personally mentoring them and uplifting their lives — many have gone on to build their own careers in photography and filmmaking.

His guidance goes beyond operating a camera, covering visual storytelling, lighting, composition, cinematography, filmmaking, creative direction and working in the real-world media industry.

“Knowledge becomes meaningful when it is shared.”
05
COVID-19 Lockdown

Chennai to Madras

When one of India's busiest cities fell unusually quiet, Ramachandran began documenting Chennai without its usual crowds, traffic and movement.

The result was Chennai to Madras, a coffee table book capturing the city's streets, architecture, landmarks and atmosphere — a visual historical record of a moment that could otherwise exist only in people's memories. As a fine art photographer, he revealed a different side of the city's architecture during an unprecedented time.

06
20 Years in the Making

Medley of Art

A mesmerising visual exploration that brings together colour theory, fine art photography, set design and costume in an ambitious multidisciplinary celebration of art.

Medley of Art represents 20 years of effort. The first copy has already sold successfully, and the second edition is being made with improved additions. The project has been recognised by the International Photography Awards, PX3 Paris and the Tokyo International Foto Awards.

07
Director · Cinematographer

From Photography to Cinema

As a Director, Cinematographer and Director of Photography, he has worked on advertising campaigns, feature films, documentaries and music-related productions.

Cinematographer of the films Saayavanam and Love and War, and the documentaries The Land of Festival and Coimbatore — Journey through Culture Landscape. Director and DoP for the TVC campaign of AVR Swarna Mahal Jewellers.

Second-unit cinematographer with Ravi Varman ISC, including the Odisha State Anthem video Bande Utkala Janani; promotional photographer for 30 films including Garudan, Teenz, Iravin Nizhal, Thaimaaman and projects connected with Indian 2.

08
Sports Photography

Documenting Sport Through Emotion

Rather than simply recording the action, he focuses on the people and emotions surrounding sport — athletes, coaches, audiences, celebrations, preparation and the defining moments that give an event its identity.

Exhibitions & Media

A Global Artistic Presence

His solo and group exhibitions have taken his work to South Korea, Singapore, Thailand, Croatia, Germany, Italy and Spain. His work has been featured in 25 magazines and forums including Playboy, Vogue, Maxim and GQ.

Milestones

Records & Honours

A Guinness World Record for Most Faces Painted (950) in January 2019, a Unique World Record and an Indian Achiever Award.

Two honorary doctorates — International Tamil University (2018) and St Andrews Theological University (2019). Best Documentary Film at the Norway Tamil Film Festival, Best Cinematographer at the Tamil Nadu Film Festival, and a National Award in the Best Jewellery category.

Public Projects

Working With Institutions

His portfolio includes assignments with Tamil Nadu Tourism, the Department of Art & Culture, Department of Archaeology, Department of Handlooms and Textiles, Greater Chennai Corporation, Madras High Court, DIPR Tamil Nadu and the HR&CE Department.

12
One Visual Language

A Creative Practice Across Mediums

Photography, filmmaking, cinematography, documentary work, publishing and art are not separate pursuits in his practice. They are different ways of telling stories.

Whether photographing an athlete in competition, documenting a centuries-old tradition, creating a cinematic frame or building a coffee table book, his work is driven by the same objective: to observe, interpret and preserve.

The Vision
