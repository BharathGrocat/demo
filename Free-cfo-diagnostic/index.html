What the complimentary diagnostic includes

Everyone says “free diagnostic.” Here's exactly what ours covers.

No vague promises. A senior finance professional works through six concrete areas of your business — and hands the findings back to you in writing.

01

Business model & performance review

We map your business model, revenue streams, cost drivers and the KPIs that actually move the needle — so you can see what's driving, and draining, the business.

02

Strategy & growth alignment

We understand your strategic goals, current challenges and growth plans, then pressure-test whether your finances are built to support them.

03

Controls & month-end close

We review internal controls, reconciliations and month-end closing procedures to surface the gaps that quietly create risk and delay.

04

Reporting, MIS & compliance

We evaluate your financial reporting, management information systems (MIS) and statutory compliance processes against best practice.

05

Compliance calendar

We prepare a compliance calendar so every statutory obligation has an owner and a date — nothing slips through the cracks.

06

SOP development

We help develop Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) so your finance function runs consistently, even as the team and business scale.
